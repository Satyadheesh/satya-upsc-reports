Pakistan named incoming chair of IMF and World Bank Group Boards for 2027 — and 4 more UPSC stories you can’t skip, 17 October 2026

1. Pakistan named incoming chair of IMF and World Bank Group Boards for 2027 (GS2)
   Pakistan was named the incoming chair of the Boards of Governors of the IMF and World Bank Group for 2027 during the 2026 WBG-IMF Annual Meetings in Bangkok.
2. India's domestic steel prices rise despite global market slowdown (GS3)
   Hareesh V of Geojit Investments Limited reported that India's domestic steel prices are rising despite a global slowdown caused by Chinese oversupply.
3. Mining and road construction threaten Rayalaseema’s 2.5-billion-year-old geological wonders (GS3)
   Geologists from Sri Venkateswara University highlighted that mining and road construction activities are threatening 2.5-billion-year-old geological sites in Rayalaseema
4. Andhra Pradesh pitched as manufacturing hub with ₹8.67 lakh crore investment (GS3)
   K. Rammohan Naidu and B.C. Janardhan Reddy pitched Andhra Pradesh as a manufacturing hub, highlighting a ₹8.67 lakh crore investment and the state's 7.8% GDP growth.
5. Kerala students to choose graduation timeline under FYUGP by 2027 (GS2)
   Students in the FYUGP programme in Keralam must decide by March 2027 whether to graduate after three years or continue for a fourth year.
